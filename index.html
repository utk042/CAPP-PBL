About

Computer Architecture & Parallel Processing (CCSE0304) · PBL Group 91 · NIET Greater Noida

A pharmacy inventory system in which every computation runs on simulated hardware. A sale is an ALU subtraction. A low-stock alert is a bit mask. A price is an IEEE 754 word. A stock scan is a program on a small CPU, run one clock at a time.

Progress

60%. Units 1–3 are implemented. Units 4–5 (cache, coherence and parallel systems) come in the final review.
